# Gen registry source-route ledger — 6 November 2026

All **212 RNBO 1.4.4 GenExpr registry entries** now have at least one authored
source scenario. **This is not 212 hardware passes or complete Gen qualification.**
Some registered entries are graph-oriented forms that reject the exploratory
function-call route. Declarations, bare values, aliases and calls retain their
actual authored forms; aliases inherit no outcome.

This snapshot matches **published short-smoke receipts only**, by exact
case/source hash.123 entries have at least one matched short-smoke hardware
pass,11 have at least one output mismatch and36 have at least one rejected
source. These groups can overlap. Remaining source-only rows may have separate
long qualification results: see [Gen qualification](gen-qualification.md),
[arithmetic](../evidence/xiao-gen-arithmetic.json) and
[logic](../evidence/xiao-gen-logic.json). A dash below means no matched
short-smoke observation in this audit, not an unsupported object or absence of
all other evidence.

The ledger is regenerated by `python3 tools/build_gen_route_inventory.py` and
verified in CI with `--check`. The [machine-readable report](../evidence/gen-registry-route-coverage.json)
retains all source/program/catalog hashes, individual result selectors, evidence
hashes, scoped conditions and callback timings. Visual Gen, external resources,
arbitrary modes/domains and one-frame production target calls remain unqualified
by this ledger. The separate RNBO inventory remains1,455 entries; these counts
must not be added to it as confirmed working objects.
