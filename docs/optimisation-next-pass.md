# Optimisation completion and next pass

The optimisation goal remains active. The implementation compiles RNBO-generated
C++ to native Xtensa code, consistent with the handover's proposed execution
model. The reviewed optimisations establish useful results for particular
generated forms; they do not establish complete RNBO compatibility, minimum
necessary precision, or a performance envelope for every admitted patch.

This audit treats the handover as a design reference. Its broader product
milestones and the current object-optimisation objective are recorded separately
below, so finishing an optimisation experiment does not silently close either.

## Admission and observed corpus

The flat `.rnbopat` importer delegates ordinary `newobj` descriptions to the
installed, version-pinned RNBO 1.4.4 XAM generator. It has no replacement DSP
object whitelist. It rejects visual `gen~`, embedded/abstraction dependencies
and unsupported saved UI classes. The manual Minimal C++ route has separate
source/runtime/service admission and can expose additional untested forms.
Strict preparation can therefore admit builtins beyond the optimisation
catalogues when the role, fixed 44100Hz/256 frames, parameter/service and budget
contracts fit. Compilation, semantic accuracy, allocation safety and target
performance are separate evidence levels.

A source inventory covers 26 repository graph files plus the actual drone, with 39
distinct operator tokens. This count includes ports, controls and aliases;
it is not 39 independently qualified DSP families. The first Main catalogue has
12 forms/39 cases, direct-input FX has 3 forms/12 cases, and wave2 has 6 forms/23
cases. Repeated runs of these cases do not expand their operator/mode coverage.

The actual drone has 58 operator instances across 20 tokens. A structural graph
walk finds 57 ancestors of its audio outputs; the remaining `/ 127.` node is not
connected to an audio-output ancestor. This graph observation does not prove
runtime dead code, absent side effects or a measurable saving. The five cycles
have scalar/fixed conversion inputs, while both comb delay inputs are signals.
Their different invariance properties must remain explicit.

The precision catalogues illustrate the scope difference: audio-v1 has 16 unique
method hashes and audio-v2 has 25, including helpers and expression methods.
Cycle hoisting recognizes five eligible hashes plus two explicitly skipped
both-signal forms. These are exact generated-body identities, not operator-family
completion counts. Double time and shared helpers must be classified by actual
call context. Conversely, a clock-like helper name does not justify per-sample
double work automatically.

## Ranked next material work

1. **Completed checkpoint; qualify the next precision candidate.** The integrated
   live-image, defined-integer pink adapter, compact-average four-window target
   comparison and 4KiB reservation control are now sealed in
   [the compiler optimisation record](../evidence/compiler-optimisations.json).
   It retains the target average cancellation failure rather than treating
   baseline-compatible output as corrected accuracy. Separately, the private
   two-literal Float32 pink-normalisation experiment observed about50.6% lower
   callback time in its target workload. Production integration/admission remains
   pending, and that observation is not a universal speed bound. Complete the
   independent target corner-domain/floating-environment diagnostic before
   extending precision-equivalence claims beyond tested normal workloads.
2. **Resolve numerical stability as a separate profile decision.** The private
   compensated-average report has 17 native cases, including longer cancellation
   runs: Neumaier's reported worst mathematical error is 2.98e-8, while Kahan
   still exceeds 2e-6 in one case. Both change the old waveform by up to two PCM
   LSB. The candidate also has a native dynamic-wrapper lifecycle/UBSan proof;
   source/reset review, wider domains and target cost remain before admission.
   This is promising Float32 research, not
   proof that doubles are never needed or a silent strict-profile correction.
3. **Profile the uncovered generated hot paths.** Build/audit representative
   wave2 and additional admitted operators on Xtensa. Resolve active callers,
   classify setup/control/audio/time operations, retain unresolved calls and
   measure complete callback cost. Review scalar filter-duration hoisting for
   a guarded production context: its private target cases already show material
   benefit, but its SR/ownership proof must not be replaced by an object-name
   rule. Keep signal conversions per sample and preserve rounding.
4. **Expand a mode-aware support inventory.** Derive the wider candidate set
   from the pinned generator/builtin surface and its service constraints, then
   add original fixtures for missing families and modes. For each record source
   generation/admission, independent native semantics, raw/optional differences,
   first-event allocation, target output/cost and rejection reasons separately.
   Begin with the unqualified modes above; continue beyond these two waves to
   collections/sequencing, spectral/FFT/convolution, resources and Gen where
   their required importer/services are implemented. A finite hash catalogue
   must not become an undocumented replacement for the compiler's broader scope.
5. **Measure the complete resource envelope.** Structural descriptor minima
   are not measured patch requirements. Record target context, arena high-water,
   loaded text/data/BSS, metadata, capture buffers and old/new coexistence peak.
   Arena exhaustion currently aborts/traps; new patches need measured admission
   before permanent-player activation. Then test composed Main+FX, UI/control,
   capture and networking loads. Isolated timing and a silent single-Main live
   run do not establish this combined deadline budget.
6. **Measure placement and vector trade-offs.** Compare code/state placement
   and cache/bus pressure under matched workloads, followed by separately
   exported 128/64-frame profiles if worthwhile. Current 256-frame hashes and
   PCM/driver/event buffers cannot qualify those vectors. Keep observed peaks
   distinct from hard bounds and keep scalar conversion-helper microbenchmarks
   separate from whole-object and whole-instrument improvements.

## Broader handover gates that remain separate

The isolated real-RNBO loader proves a native loading route. The permanent
audio/UI shell still needs live dynamic replacement and independent Main→FX
lifecycle/routing, bypass/tails, peak-memory admission and failure recovery.
Durable versioned packages/settings, interrupted installation/reboot recovery,
browser conversion/deployment, authenticated network/group control and fuller
importer/Gen coverage remain product work. Shared polyphonic effects require
their intended topology rather than unmeasured replication per voice.

AMOLED results do not qualify XIAO hardware. XIAO has compiled profiles but still
needs stereo output/capture, controls and whole-system resource/timing evidence.
Both boards ultimately need documented support and performance envelopes. This
matches the handover's compatibility/optimisation exit gate and keeps the
original objective broader than the successful examples already collected.
